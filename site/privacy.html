Reel It In Stop peeling your eyes. Give ’em a break.

Privacy policy

Last updated 20 November 2026

Reel It In is an Android app that blocks Instagram Reels. This policy explains what the app can see and what it does with it. In short: it collects nothing, and nothing leaves your phone.

What the app reads

Reel It In uses Android's AccessibilityService API. Its accessibility service is limited to the Instagram app: Android does not pass it events from any other app. To know when you leave Instagram, it also checks which app is in front, by package name only; it does not read other apps' screens. It reads the structure of Instagram's screen (view ids, labels and visible text) only to tell whether a reel is showing and where it was opened from: the Reels tab, Home, Search & Explore, your profile, or a direct message. When a reel should be blocked, the service covers the screen and goes back, using the same accessibility API.

The service does not record your messages, does not record what you type, and does not take screenshots.

What is stored, and where

  • Your settings: which places to block, how the block screen looks, and whether the notification shows.
  • A photo, only if you choose one for the block screen. A copy is kept in the app's private storage.
  • A short detection log of recent screens the app classified, kept in the app's private storage to help diagnose problems. An entry can include the username of a reel's author, which is how the app tells one reel from the next. The log is never sent anywhere. You can clear it, or export it yourself from the app.

All of this stays on your device. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

What is shared

Nothing. The app does not ask for internet access, so it cannot send data off your phone. It has no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no accounts.

Permissions

  • Accessibility service: to detect and block reels in Instagram, as described above. You turn it on and off in Android's settings.
  • Notifications: to show whether blocking is on, with a Pause 1 min button. You can hide it in the app.
  • Battery optimization exemption: so Android does not stop the blocker in the background. Optional.

Children

The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
