Instagram, without the Reels.

Reel It In blocks Instagram Reels on Android. The reel a friend sends you still plays. Swipe to the next one and it's blocked.

Reel blocked

You choose where

  • Reels tabThe endless feed. Blocked the moment it opens.
  • HomeReels in your feed are hidden as you scroll.
  • Search & ExploreReels you open there, even the long-press preview.
  • Your profileYour own reels, posts and reposts. Allowed unless you say otherwise.
  • DMsWatch the reel a friend sent you. Swiping on is blocked.

Calm by design

  • Your block screenA color, a quiet pattern, or a photo of your own.
  • Pause 1 minOne tap in the notification. Blocking comes back by itself.
  • Nothing in the wayEvery cover is gone the moment you leave Instagram.

Private

Reel It In has no internet permission. It can't send anything anywhere.

It uses Android's accessibility service to read Instagram's screen, and only Instagram's, to tell when a reel is on it. What it sees stays on your phone. Privacy policy
